Request and quote. A request sent from the "Start a project" page does not commit you. If I can make the piece, I email you a quote with the total price, the estimated making time and the deposit amount. The "from" price shown on the site is a starting price: only the quoted price counts. The quote is valid for 30 days.

This is a down payment ("acompte"), not "arrhes", within the meaning of article L214-1 of the French Consumer Code: the order is firm and final for both of us. Before paying, you expressly accept these terms (checkbox). The deposit starts the work. You receive a deposit invoice.

No right of withdrawal. A piece made to your specifications or clearly personalised gives no right of withdrawal (article L221-28 3° of the French Consumer Code). The legal guarantee of conformity and the guarantee against hidden defects (section 6) apply as usual.

Approval from photos. When the piece is finished, I email you photos. You approve it from your request page, or ask for a change. 1 change is included in the price (adjusting the piece or the design, within the accepted quote); further changes, or changes to the quote, are quoted separately.

Balance before shipping. The balance (total price minus the deposit) and shipping, calculated for your country, are paid online after your approval and before shipping. Paying the balance counts as approval. The piece then ships like a normal order (order number, tracking, invoice showing the deposit already paid).

If I don't hear from you. If you neither approve the piece nor pay the balance, I send you a reminder after 7 days. Without approval or payment of the balance 30 days after the photos are sent (reminder included), the order is considered abandoned by you: vgthmind keeps the deposit as compensation for the work done, and the piece remains mine.

If I decline or abandon. If I decline your request after the deposit is paid, or cannot complete the piece, the deposit is refunded in full, to the original payment method, within 14 days.

Making time. The quoted time is an estimate. If delivery is more than 30 days later than the announced date (not counting delays due to a change request or a late reply from you), you may cancel the order and get a full refund, deposit included.

Rental, visual productions and collabs. No online payment: I reply by email with a quote and, depending on the project, a contract and a deposit cheque. These specific terms take precedence over these terms of sale.

Photos of the piece. With your consent (optional box in the form), I may show photos of the finished piece on the vgthmind website and social media. You can withdraw this consent at any time by email.
