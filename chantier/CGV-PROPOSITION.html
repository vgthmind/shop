L221-28 du Code de la consommation). Ce sera toujours indiqué avant que tu paies. You may cancel your purchase without giving a reason within 14 days of receiving the piece. Email contact@vgthmind.org, then send the piece back within the following 14 days, unworn, unwashed and in its original condition. Please return it in its original packaging, preferably by tracked and insured parcel (I cannot be responsible for an untracked parcel lost on the way back). Return shipping is paid by you. I refund the order (standard shipping included) within 14 days of being told of your decision, to the original payment method, once the return arrives and is checked (I confirm receipt and my decision by email).

Custom pieces. Pieces made to measure, to order or personalised at your request (customs, painting made at your request) cannot be returned or refunded (art. L221-28, French Consumer Code). This is always stated before you pay. Optional goodwill gesture (your call): for a custom piece that truly does not suit you, offer an exchange or a one-year store credit.

Whatever happens with withdrawal, the legal guarantee of conformity (2 years) and the guarantee against hidden defects apply. A piece received defective or damaged is repaired, exchanged or refunded: email me with a photo.
