VGTHMIND is a brand whose entire process happens in France. Every garment and accessory is designed and made by me alone.

Making a garment or accessory involves all these steps:

-Design/Patternmaking: sketches, prototyping, draping, pattern-making, etc.

-Sourcing materials: local suppliers who are luxury brands clearing out excess fabric rolls, and/or reclaiming fabric scraps/garments, etc.

-Construction: cutting the pieces, assembling, sewing, finishing, ironing, etc.

As a result, each piece is a one-of-a-kind model, meant to be kept and considered a wearable piece of art.
