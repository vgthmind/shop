PERSONAL DATA

Information collected when ordering (name, address, email) is used solely to process and deliver your order. No data is shared with third parties beyond the necessary providers (carrier, payment). You have the right to access, correct and delete your data — contact me via Instagram DM to exercise it. Payments are processed by Stripe, order emails are sent through Resend, and visits are measured with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookie and does not identify you. Order data is kept for the time needed to handle the order and for the legal accounting retention period (10 years). To exercise your rights, you can also write to vgthm66@gmail.com.

LEGAL BASIS, RECIPIENTS & TRANSFERS

Your data is processed to perform the sales contract (taking payment, preparing and delivering the order) and to meet legal obligations (accounting). Only the providers needed for this receive it: Stripe (payment), Resend (order e-mails), Cloudflare (order service and anonymous visit statistics), GitHub (site hosting), and the carrier for delivery. Some of them are based in, or may process data in, the United States; these transfers rely on the safeguards provided by the GDPR (standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework). Watching a video on a product page loads Instagram (Meta) only after you click “play”. If you think your rights are not respected, you can complain to the CNIL (cnil.fr).
