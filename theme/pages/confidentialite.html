WHAT WE COLLECT, AND WHY

Orders. Name, delivery address, e-mail and the items bought are used solely to take payment, prepare and deliver your order, and send you order e-mails. Payment card details are entered on Stripe’s page and never reach this site. Order data is kept for the legal accounting retention period (10 years).

Contact form. Name, e-mail, subject and message are used only to answer you. They arrive by e-mail at contact@vgthmind.org (sent through Resend) and are deleted at the latest 12 months after our last exchange. To limit spam, the site briefly counts how many messages come from the same connection (a few hours, nothing else is stored from it).

“Start a project” requests (custom, made to measure, visual production, collab). Your answers (name, e-mail, country, description, references, colours, size or measurements, budget, dates…) and the photos or videos you send (8 photos of 10 MB and 1 video of 50 MB maximum) are used only to study your request, send you a quote and make the piece. They are stored by Cloudflare (Durable Objects and R2 file storage, European Union and United States) and reach me by e-mail (Resend). For a deposit or a balance, the payment goes through Stripe, and I keep the date and wording of the conditions you accepted. How long: a request with no follow-up, declined or completed is deleted 12 months after our last exchange; your files are deleted 6 months after the request ends; the photos of the finished piece are kept only if you agreed to their use (optional box, which you can withdraw at any time by e-mail); invoices and payments are kept 10 years (accounting obligation). Your private request link is never shown to anyone else.

Visit statistics. Cloudflare Web Analytics measures visits without any cookie and without identifying you. No advertising, no tracking cookies.

LEGAL BASIS, RECIPIENTS & TRANSFERS

Orders: performance of the sales contract and legal accounting obligations. Forms and requests: your request (pre-contractual steps, then the contract for a paid piece); photos of the finished piece shown online: your consent and my legitimate interest in answering you. Statistics: legitimate interest (anonymous audience measurement). Recipients are only the providers needed: Stripe (payment), Resend (e-mails), Cloudflare (order service, anonymous statistics), GitHub (site hosting), and the carrier (delivery). Some are based in, or may process data in, the United States; these transfers rely on the safeguards provided by the GDPR (standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework). Watching a video on a product page loads Instagram (Meta) only after you click “play”.

YOUR RIGHTS

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its use, by writing to contact@vgthmind.org. If you think your rights are not respected, you can complain to the CNIL (cnil.fr).
