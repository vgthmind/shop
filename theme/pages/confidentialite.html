WHAT WE COLLECT, AND WHY

Orders. Name, delivery address, e-mail and the items bought are used solely to take payment, prepare and deliver your order, and send you order e-mails. Payment card details are entered on Stripe’s page and never reach this site. Order data is kept for the legal accounting retention period (10 years).

Contact and rental / collab forms. Name, e-mail, subject and message (plus project details and dates for a rental or collab request) are used only to answer you. They arrive by e-mail at contact@vgthmind.org (sent through Resend) and are deleted at the latest 12 months after our last exchange. To limit spam, the site briefly counts how many messages come from the same connection (a few hours, nothing else is stored from it).

Visit statistics. Cloudflare Web Analytics measures visits without any cookie and without identifying you. No advertising, no tracking cookies.

LEGAL BASIS, RECIPIENTS & TRANSFERS

Orders: performance of the sales contract and legal accounting obligations. Forms: your request (pre-contractual steps) and my legitimate interest in answering you. Statistics: legitimate interest (anonymous audience measurement). Recipients are only the providers needed: Stripe (payment), Resend (e-mails), Cloudflare (order service, anonymous statistics), GitHub (site hosting), and the carrier (delivery). Some are based in, or may process data in, the United States; these transfers rely on the safeguards provided by the GDPR (standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework). Watching a video on a product page loads Instagram (Meta) only after you click “play”.

YOUR RIGHTS

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its use, by writing to contact@vgthmind.org. If you think your rights are not respected, you can complain to the CNIL (cnil.fr).
